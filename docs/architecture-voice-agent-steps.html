FloorWorks intake - voice-agent mode, step by step

TRANSCRIPTION_MODE=voice-agent. The yellow step is what differs from acs-stt mode.
Phase 1 - AI receptionist on the call
1
Customer calls the phone number CustomerAzure Communication Services receives the call and notifies the VoiceIntake app.
2
VoiceIntake answers and opens the audio stream VoiceIntakeACS streams the caller's audio to the app over a WebSocket.
3
VoiceIntake connects to the Foundry voice agent Voice LiveCaller audio goes in; the agent's spoken replies come back and are played to the caller.
4
The agent greets and collects the details Voice agentIt asks who is calling, which equipment, and what is wrong. It can look things up in Dataverse through the MCP toolbox.
5
The agent's own transcript of the caller is used Voice agentThe voice agent returns what it heard. VoiceIntake adds it to the shared transcript. No separate speech-to-text runs for the caller.
6
Note-taker turns the transcript into a draft Note-taker agentCustomer, equipment and issue are saved as a draft in Dataverse and appear live on the rep console. No ticket exists yet.
Handoff - AI to human
7
Customer asks for a person Voice agentThe agent calls the transfer_to_rep tool. VoiceIntake asks ACS to ring the rep in Teams.
8
Agent says goodbye and goes silent VoiceIntakeThe AI is muted, and speech-to-text now starts for the customer, because the agent no longer hears them.
Phase 2 - Rep on the call
9
Rep answers in Teams RepACS speech-to-text transcribes the customer and the rep separately. The note-taker keeps updating the draft.
10
Rep reviews and edits the draft Rep consoleThey confirm the customer and equipment (suggestions are shown) and fix any details. Their edits are never overwritten.
11
Rep clicks Submit RepOnly now is the real service ticket created in Dataverse, linked to the customer and equipment.
